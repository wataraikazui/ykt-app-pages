About Monodachi

Monodachi: Things Journal helps you organize the things you care about and keep each thing’s use, care, repairs, memories, warranties, receipts and manuals together.

Getting started

1. Tap “Welcome” and add a photo and name. 2. Open the thing from its circle. 3. Tap “Used” or “Add a note” to build its history.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos and records stored?
They are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer’s servers.

Does “Typical lifespan” predict when something will fail?
No. It only compares the number of years you enter with the time you have spent with the thing.

How do I move to a new device?
Export a backup from Settings, then import it on your new device.

Can I edit or hide the use count?
Open “Times used” on the detail screen to edit the number, hide it or return to the automatic count.

How do I remove ads?
Open Settings and choose “Remove Ads.” If you purchased it before, use “Restore Purchases.”
