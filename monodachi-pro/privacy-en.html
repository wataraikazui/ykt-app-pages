Monodachi Privacy Policy

This policy explains how YKT (“we”) handles information in Monodachi: Things Journal (“the app”).

1. Information stored by the app

Names, dates, prices, journal entries, photos, settings and backup information entered by you are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or send this content to our servers.

2. Photo access

The app accesses your photo library only when you choose photos for things, journal entries or documents. Photos you select are stored and displayed in the app and are not sent to our servers. Photos taken with the in-app camera may also be saved to your photo library when you allow it.

3. Backups and sharing

You can export a backup and send it through the system share sheet. You choose and control the destination and content. We do not receive your backup.

4. Advertising

The English interface may use Google AdMob for advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, crash logs and performance data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party disclosure

We do not provide your locally stored records or photos to third parties. Google may process data through the AdMob SDK as described above.

6. In-app purchase

The app may offer a one-time purchase to remove the entire ad area. Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be published on this page.
